How can we help?

GigSlip keeps income, expenses and receipts together on your iPhone or iPad.

Service country and state

GigSlip’s service country is fixed to the United States and its currency is USD. Choose your US state during setup and change it in settings. App language is independent of your state. Selecting a state does not enable verified tax calculations in the current beta.

Add income or an expense

Use the income/expense entry action, enter the amount, choose a category and save. Attaching a receipt is optional. On a narrow window the editor opens from the bottom; on a wide iPad window it opens on the right.

Check a receipt amount

Attach a receipt with the camera or photo picker. If an amount is suggested, compare it with the image and choose “Use amount” to apply it. If no suggestion appears, enter the amount manually. A printed pre-tip amount may differ from the final payment. OCR suggestions are not guaranteed to be correct.

Find and edit records

Open the records list, search or filter the year, type or category, and open a record to see its details or edit it. Receipt images remain linked to their record.

Send documents to an accountant

Open the year-end screen and select the year. “Export for accountant” creates one ZIP with an Excel ledger, a numbered receipt PDF and original image attachments. Match record 0001 in Excel with receipt 0001 in the PDF and attachment 0001. Missing images are explicitly shown. Long receipts continue on additional PDF pages.

Back up or restore a year

Use the year-backup export action to save a .gigslip file to a location you control. Restore through the backup import action, review the selected year and confirm before replacing that year’s records. An accountant ZIP or PDF report is not a restorable app backup. No cloud synchronization is provided.

Tax calculations and reserve tracking

This beta provides bookkeeping and manually managed tax-reserve records. Verified federal and 50-state tax calculations, tax savings and filing are not currently provided. Exported ledgers are not filed tax returns.

Update the app or leave a review

App updates are installed by Apple or the App Store, according to your settings. The app’s settings provide version checks and a store review link when a public listing is available. An unpublished TestFlight app may not have a public store listing.
