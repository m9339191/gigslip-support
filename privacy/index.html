Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Applies to GigSlip 0.7

Your financial records stay on your device. You decide when to export or share them.

Records and receipt images

Income, expenses, receipt images, notes, categories, tax-reserve entries and preferences are stored in the app’s local storage. GigSlip does not operate an account, bank connection, financial-record upload service or cloud synchronization service.

Receipt recognition

Receipt text recognition uses Apple Vision on your device. The app does not send your receipt images, recognized text or transaction amounts to a remote AI model. Suggestions can be incorrect: review the original receipt and confirm an amount before using it. The app does not automatically determine business purpose or tax deductibility.

Permissions

Camera and photo access are requested when you choose receipt capture or an image. You can enter records manually if access is unavailable. File selection and sharing use platform controls.

Exports and backups

Exports are created when you request them. A CSV, Excel ledger, PDF receipt package, original image attachment or year backup may contain private financial information. These exported files are not password encrypted. The app passes them to the destination you select using the system share sheet. That destination’s privacy terms apply.

Sharing a file is not proof that a backup was saved. Keep a separate year backup in a location you control. GigSlip’s ledger is excluded from automatic iCloud backup; do not rely on iCloud device backup as a copy of your ledger.

Apple services and network requests

Version checks contact Apple’s lookup service with the app’s store identifier and storefront country when a valid store listing is configured. Apple receives ordinary network information such as an IP address. Financial records and receipt content are not included. Reviews and app installation or updates are handled by Apple and the App Store. Apple’s privacy terms apply to its services.

Advertising

GigSlip is free and shows a small number of ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The app requests non-personalized ads only and does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt or the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app identifiers, ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic and performance information. Google’s privacy policy and advertising terms apply to that processing. Your income and expense records, receipt images, recognized text, categories and tax profile are never sent to advertising services or used for ad targeting. Where a regional privacy law requires it, an advertising privacy choices control appears in Settings using Google’s User Messaging Platform.

Remove Ads purchase

A one-time Remove Ads purchase (no subscription) permanently turns off all advertising and stops ad requests. The purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store; GigSlip receives only Apple’s verified entitlement on your device and does not receive your payment details. Restore purchases is available on the same screen. All bookkeeping, search, backup, restore and export features stay free without the purchase.

Tracking and analytics

GigSlip does not include a developer analytics service, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell financial records.

Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them or remove the app. A deletion in GigSlip does not remove copies you previously exported, emailed or saved elsewhere. Uninstalling the app can remove its local records. The developer cannot restore a ledger that has no usable backup.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request. Do not send a receipt, tax identifier, bank details or a full backup unless necessary for the issue. Public research documents used during model development are separate from personal app records; your app records are not automatically contributed to training.

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests under its privacy statement. The site has no analytics script or sign-in.

Changes and contact

We update this page when the app’s data practices change and show the effective date above. Contact GigSlip support at it@bebriz.com.

Tóm tắt tiếng Việt

Hồ sơ tài chính, ảnh biên lai và OCR được xử lý trên thiết bị. Ứng dụng không gửi biên lai đến máy chủ nhà phát triển hoặc AI từ xa. Bạn chủ động xuất và chia sẻ dữ liệu. Tệp xuất không mã hóa bằng mật khẩu; hãy lưu bản sao lưu theo năm và không dựa vào sao lưu iCloud để giữ sổ. Ứng dụng miễn phí và hiển thị một số quảng cáo không cá nhân hóa qua Google AdMob. Để phân phối, đo lường quảng cáo và chống gian lận, Google có thể xử lý địa chỉ IP, mã định danh thiết bị/ứng dụng, tương tác với quảng cáo và thông tin chẩn đoán; sổ sách, biên lai và hồ sơ thuế không được dùng cho quảng cáo. Mua một lần “Gỡ quảng cáo” (không đăng ký định kỳ) do Apple xử lý và tắt mọi quảng cáo vĩnh viễn. Không có dịch vụ phân tích của nhà phát triển hay theo dõi giữa các ứng dụng. Email hỗ trợ, dịch vụ Apple và trang GitHub tuân theo việc xử lý của từng dịch vụ.

Resumen en español

Los registros financieros, las imágenes y el OCR se procesan en el dispositivo. La app no envía recibos al servidor del desarrollador ni a una IA remota. Tú decides cuándo exportar y compartir. Los archivos exportados no están protegidos con contraseña; guarda copias anuales y no dependas de iCloud para conservar el libro. La app es gratuita y muestra algunos anuncios no personalizados mediante Google AdMob. Para mostrar y medir anuncios y prevenir fraudes, Google puede procesar tu dirección IP, identificadores del dispositivo y de la app, interacciones con anuncios e información de diagnóstico; los registros, recibos y el perfil fiscal no se usan para publicidad. La compra única «Quitar anuncios» (sin suscripción) la procesa Apple y desactiva toda la publicidad de forma permanente. No hay servicio de análisis del desarrollador ni seguimiento entre apps. El correo de soporte, Apple y GitHub tienen sus propios procesos.
