Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Applies to GigSlip 0.7

Your financial records stay on your device. You decide when to export or share them.

Records and receipt images

Income, expenses, receipt images, notes, categories, tax-reserve entries and preferences are stored in the app’s local storage. GigSlip does not operate an account, bank connection, financial-record upload service or cloud synchronization service.

Receipt recognition

Receipt text recognition uses Apple Vision on your device. The app does not send your receipt images, recognized text or transaction amounts to a remote AI model. Suggestions can be incorrect: review the original receipt and confirm an amount before using it. The app does not automatically determine business purpose or tax deductibility.

Permissions

Camera and photo access are requested when you choose receipt capture or an image. You can enter records manually if access is unavailable. File selection and sharing use platform controls.

Exports and backups

Exports are created when you request them. A CSV, Excel ledger, PDF receipt package, original image attachment or year backup may contain private financial information. These exported files are not password encrypted. The app passes them to the destination you select using the system share sheet. That destination’s privacy terms apply.

Sharing a file is not proof that a backup was saved. Keep a separate year backup in a location you control. GigSlip’s ledger is excluded from automatic iCloud backup; do not rely on iCloud device backup as a copy of your ledger.

Apple services and network requests

Version checks contact Apple’s lookup service with the app’s store identifier and storefront country when a valid store listing is configured. Apple receives ordinary network information such as an IP address. Financial records and receipt content are not included. Reviews and app installation or updates are handled by Apple and the App Store. Apple’s privacy terms apply to its services.

Advertising, tracking and analytics

This version includes no advertising SDK, behavioral tracking or developer analytics service. We do not sell financial records or send receipt data to advertising services.

Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them or remove the app. A deletion in GigSlip does not remove copies you previously exported, emailed or saved elsewhere. Uninstalling the app can remove its local records. The developer cannot restore a ledger that has no usable backup.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request. Do not send a receipt, tax identifier, bank details or a full backup unless necessary for the issue. Public research documents used during model development are separate from personal app records; your app records are not automatically contributed to training.

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests under its privacy statement. The site has no analytics script or sign-in.

Changes and contact

We update this page when the app’s data practices change and show the effective date above. Contact GigSlip support at it@bebriz.com.
